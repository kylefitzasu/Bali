Essential Tips

🚇
Octopus Card
Buy at any MTR station. Covers metro, buses, Star Ferry, and 7-Eleven.
💰
Money
Carry HKD cash for markets and street food. Cards work everywhere upscale.
🌡️
Weather
Light, breathable clothes. Bring a layer for aggressive restaurant AC.
📱
Apps
Download Citymapper HK. Google Translate camera works great here.

Kyle & Sarah Recommended Shortlist

🏙️
Sham Shui Po
Best stop for the crowded borough / vertical-city feel, local food, fabrics, electronics, neon, and real Hong Kong texture.
📸
Monster Building
Iconic stacked residential towers in Quarry Bay. Quick photo stop, but be respectful because it is residential.
🎨
Tai Kwun + PMQ
The best art/design/cocktail/architecture pairing near Central and Sheung Wan.
🖼️
M+ Museum
Modern visual culture, excellent architecture, and a better fit than Avenue of Stars for art and design.
🍸
COA / Dragonfly
Strong cocktail options if you want a more adult, design-forward Hong Kong night.
🍽️
Duddell's
Great alternative to Lung King Heen: modern Cantonese, artful atmosphere, and less formal than a hotel fine-dining room.

Local Tips & Spots

💰
Currency & Money
Hong Kong uses HKD. Bali uses Indonesian Rupiah. Carry some cash for markets and street food. Cards work well at hotels and upscale restaurants.
🛺
Getting Around
In Bali, use Grab or Gojek for reliable rides. In Hong Kong, use the MTR and Octopus Card.
🍜
Food & Water
In Bali, stick to bottled water. In Hong Kong, street food and dim sum are a must.
🛕
Temple Etiquette
In Bali, wear a sarong and sash to enter temples. Cover shoulders and knees.
🌺
Must-See Near Nusa Dua
Uluwatu Temple at sunset, Jimbaran Bay seafood, Tanah Lot, Seminyak beach clubs, and Tegallalang rice terraces.
🕐
Time Zone
Bali is UTC+8. Denver in May is MDT, so Bali is 14 hours ahead.
